# TuringCorp Team Senior v1

Model ID: `turingcorp/team-senior-v1` · Product: Team · Tier: Senior
Status: **Preview**

## What it is

Deeper reasoning with enhanced quality for important decisions. Same collaborative design as Team
Junior — independent reasoning paths cross-examined before delivery, answer delivered as
*content + reason* — running a heavier configuration.
